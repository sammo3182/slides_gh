What We Know, and What We Don’t

Known: inequality is bad

On the social fabric (Cozzolino 2011; Payne et al. 2017)

  • ↑ risk-taking, obesity, teenage births
  • ↓ interpersonal trust, child well-being, life expectancy

On social cohesion (Rothstein 2005; Uslaner 2008)

  • ↓ social capital and the basis of consensus

Unknown: why citizens don’t ask for more

左栏是三十年文献的共识：不平等对社会肌理、对民主政治、对社会凝聚力都是负向的。

右栏是缺口。Whyte 的长期调查显示中国公众对不平等的容忍主要来自功绩制信念、发展叙事与上升预期 (Whyte 2010, 2016)。但“为什么容忍”这个问题，文献大多直接断言一条从不平等到态度的直线，很少去找中间步骤。

Method involved

No experiment, no DID, no RD, no synthetic control. Regression-ish methods only.

三项任务对应三个板块，也对应三篇已发表的工作。

要提前说明第三点的分寸：这不是说普通回归可以替代实验设计，而是说在实验不可得的领域，推断的可信度来自设计阶段的选择、估计阶段的纪律、以及主动的推翻检验，而不是估计量本身的名气。

What

What does inequality shape?

Two Theories, Opposite Predictions

  • Conflict theory: under high inequality, the poor gain class consciousness ↑ and reject meritocracy ↑
    • Premise: social structures are self-negating
  • Relative power theory: the poor do not gain class consciousness, and believe in meritocracy more
    • Premise: social structures reproduce themselves (Goodin and Dryzek 1980)

Class: bourgeoisie, proletariat。功绩制信念是美国梦的核心，也是穷人希望的来源。

Conflict theory：暴露于本地不平等提高穷人对自身劣势位置的感知，激发阶级意识。对手论文的原话是，这“increases the salience of their disadvantaged position within a conspicuous local economic hierarchy”。

Relative power theory：金钱是政治资源。不平等越大，富人越有能力在公共领域传播自己的观点，穷人越缺乏抵抗的资源，于是内化强者的价值与规则。

“When Statisticians Lie”

Newman, Benjamin J., Christopher D. Johnston, and Patrick L. Lown. 2015. “False Consciousness or Class Awareness? Local Income Inequality, Personal Economic Position, and Belief in American Meritocracy.” American Journal of Political Science 59(2): 326–40.

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{Reject}_{ij} = {}& \mathbf{X}\beta + \beta_{10}\text{Income}_{ij} + \color{red}{\beta_{01}}\text{Inequality}_j \\ & + \color{red}{\beta_{11}}(\text{Inequality}_j \times \text{Income}_{ij}) + u_{ij} \end{aligned} \]

\[\frac{\partial\ \text{Reject}_{ij}}{\partial\ \text{Inequality}_j} = \color{red}{\beta_{01}} + \color{red}{\beta_{11}}\cdot\text{Income}_{ij}\]

NJL reads \(\hat\beta_{01}\) and \(\hat\beta_{11}\) directly as proof that the effect of inequality reverses sign across income.

\(\beta_{01}\) alone is the effect only at Income \(=0\) — but in NJL’s data Income\(_{ij}\in[0.21,\,1]\), never zero. A coefficient is not a conditional effect.

What the wrong interpretation looks like

模拟数据，仅作示意，不是复现 NJL 的实际估计（N = 3000，seed 86）。

交互项系数显著：β̂ = −0.31，p = .040。按 NJL 的读法，调节效应就此成立。

左图看边际效应。不平等取最低值时，收入的条件效应是 −0.99，95% CI 为 [−2.12, 0.13]。取最高值时是 −2.05，CI 为 [−3.20, −0.91]。两个区间重叠了较窄区间宽度的约 54%，而且每一端的点估计都落在另一端的置信区间内。把两端的条件效应当作两个独立的分布来比较，z = −1.29，p = .20，差异不显著。从最平等到最不平等，收入效应的变化远不如交互项的 p 值看上去那么确定。

右图用 predPro 画预测概率。不平等取最低值和最高值时的两条曲线，在整个收入区间内都重叠。

为什么会这样：收入与一个 SES 控制变量高度相关（r ≈ .96），收入自身的效应因此估计得很不精确，边际效应的置信带很宽。交互项的系数却不受这个共线性影响，照样显著。所以解读调节效应必须看边际效应和它的置信区间，不能只看交互项系数的显著性。

备问：如果展示回归表，ses 的系数（+0.61）与真实值（−0.4）符号相反，这是共线性所致。另外，interplot 的 CI(Max − Min) 由 Δz × β̂ 抽样得到，与交互项的 Wald 检验等价，本例中为 [−2.09, −0.03]。所以图上不显示 stats_cp。

“How Data Lie”

Do It Right

第一条是全场方法论的主线。正确做法是对模型求偏导得到条件效应，并在所有观测到的收入取值上计算。Brambor、Clark 与 Golder 的原话是：交互项的构成项“indicates only the effect of Inequality when the other variable in the interaction, Income, takes on the value of zero”，而且“cannot even infer whether X has a meaningful conditional effect on Y from the magnitude and significance of the coefficient on the interaction term”。

第二条：三种测量分别是 2005、2006 的二择一题，2007 的两句四点量表同时同意，2009 的只取其中一句。1999 至 2012 年所有 Pew 调查的时间序列显示三者水平差异巨大，原文的判断是“pooling them into a single analysis cannot be justified”，结论一句更狠：“The results presented in NJL are an artifact generated by the decision to pool these three very different measures together.”

第三条：对手称那些题项是 2006 年调查独有，实际上“were not at all unique to the 2006 survey: they were in fact included in six Pew surveys during the period examined in the study, 2005 to 2009”。于是 2017 的结论是“In neglecting to examine more than a single survey, NJL reaches a conclusion that is not supported by the available evidence”。

Wrap up

  • Higher inequality → the poor:
    • Reject meritocracy less
    • Less likely to gain class consciousness

2016 的预测概率：最低收入组从观测到的最低不平等水平移到最高水平，拒绝功绩制的概率下降 19 个百分点（正负 7）；4 万至 5 万美元组从 37%（正负 2）降到 30%（正负 3）；最高收入组在整个不平等区间内与零无异。

2017 的三个面板：收入本身的条件效应在整个不平等区间显著为负，且随不平等升高而增大；不平等的系数只在收入高于 5 万美元的人群中显著，与对手的预测完全相反；最低收入组的 have-not 自我认同预测概率在不平等区间上是平的。

结论的重量在这里：“the current social structure will not simply undermine itself”。改变只能来自有组织的努力，原文说“will only result from concerted effort, from the difficult and much-constrained work of organization and mobilization”。

How

How does macro inequality reach the micro mind?

Is your world economically equal or not?

How do you know?

Common measures

  • S80/S20 Ratio: The richest 20% / the poorest 20%
  • Palma Ratio: The richest 10% / the poorest 40%
  • Gini Coefficient: The cumulative percentage of total income / the cumulative percentage of the population, starting from the poorest

Each measure looks at a different slice of the distribution, which is exactly why they can disagree about the same society.

Illustration

The measures agree on the ranking here because the simulation is clean. Real distributions rarely are.

Output Evaluation vs. Mediator Evaluation

Output Evaluation Model

Inequality is a system output; citizens evaluate it and blame the government (Easton 1965)

Four hidden assumptions

  1. Perceive inequality accurately
  2. Regard it as important
  3. Hold retrospective views
  4. Attribute blame to government

Mediator Evaluation Model (Jin et al. 2024)

Citizens do not evaluate inequality; they experience it

Less demanding

  • Easier to judge how government treated you than how unequal society is
  • Multiple paths, one is enough

Not the “perceived inequality” turn

Relaxing accurate perception still assumes citizens consider inequality important (Gimpelson and Treisman 2018)

这是该文的核心理论贡献，也是原版幻灯片没有讲的部分。

OEM 的四个假设即便在信息充分的民主国家也备受挑战。MEM 的主张是：“as long as high inequality exists, the mediation processes link higher inequality to lower political trust, regardless of whether this inequality is understood.”

重要的分寸：作者明确反对退回到“只有被正确感知的不平等才有后果”。客观不平等依然是分析焦点，因为它强有力地塑造社会条件，只是作用方式是间接的。

People can feel inequality in various ways (Jin et al. 2024)

Impartial governance:

  • The damage of economic disparities on political equality in democracies has been well documented in the literature (Schlozman et al. 2013).
  • Formal and informal institutions exist in non-democracies to convert economic resources into political influence and favorable treatment.
  • Wealthy Chinese private entrepreneurs form extensive connections with local government officials to gain privileges (Chen and Dickson 2010).

Government Non-responsiveness:

  • Substantial evidence indicates that economic inequality reduces social capital and generates distrust and social conflicts.
  • Class conflict theory suggests that in highly unequal societies, people’s perceptions of economic positions on the social ladder are accentuated (Kraus et al. 2013).
  • Low levels of social capital and accentuated social conflicts make governance difficult: Baldwin and Huber (2010) showed that income differences make agreement on which public goods to provide more difficult, and therefore have a negative relationship with public goods provision (Epp and Jennings 2020).

Judicial Injustice:

  1. Greater inequality implies that the well-off have more resources to control the legal apparatus while the poor are more disadvantaged.
  2. The advantaged are motivated to attempt such control because of intensified social conflicts.

Anti-Corruption Inadequacy:

When inequality leads to widespread corruption, citizens doubt governments’ intentions and abilities to combat corruption and perceive governments as incompetent to address the problems of the system.

Methodological Summary

Regression-based “causality”—on what grounds?

Tip 1: Design Before Estimation

Credibility starts with how the data are chosen, not with the estimator

Temporal ordering

Inequality measured in 2013 → attitudes measured in 2015

  • Reverse causation ruled out by design
  • Not corrected at the estimation stage

Independent sources

CHFS 2013 → treatment CUGS 2015 → mediators and outcomes

  • Avoids common-source bias

时序在先这一条，作者在正文和附录里各写了一遍：“We avoid this issue to affect the results through data source selection”，以及“we handle this issue through the design phase”。要强调这是一个设计选择，不是事后补救。

同时必须诚实：这只解决了处理到中介这一段。中介与信任都来自 2015 年同一份调查，那一段的内生性仍在，要靠后面的 2SLS 去压。

Tip 2: Discipline in Estimation

Use your methods right

  • Do not only focus on the asterisks
  • Compute conditional effects across observed values (Brambor et al. 2006)
  • Report how much of the sample that range covers (Berry et al. 2012)

Modeling the reality

  • All four mediators enter one model
  • Each path estimated net of the other three
  • Not four separate models shown side by side

“同时进入”是一个保守选择。四个中介之间确实相关，Kendall’s tau 最高的一对是公正治理与政治效能的 0.62。把它们放进同一个模型会互相吃掉解释力，公正治理的路径很可能就是这样落空的。

作者的处理是透明的：正文保留更保守的全中介设定，附录报告单中介设定。单中介设定下四条路径全部显著，而且总效应也达到显著。他们没有把附录那一版挪到正文来。

这一页的落点：估计阶段的纪律不是为了让结果好看，恰恰是为了让结果更难好看。

Tip 3: Being Scientific

Do the sensitivity analysis

Channel Central Local
Government responsiveness 0.10 0.15
Impartial governance 0.10 0.05
Judicial credibility 0.20 0.20
Anti-corruption effectiveness 0.25 0.40
  • The strongest channel is also the most robust; on the weakest, the authors decline to draw a conclusion
  • 2SLS against reverse causation: the forward effect is about three times the backward one
  • Province fixed effects, single-mediator models, income subgroups: conclusions unchanged

表中的数是 rho 的临界值：误差项相关达到这个水平时，中介效应变得与零无异 (Imai, Keele, and Yamamoto 2010; Imai et al. 2011)。

没有公认的门槛。作者的对照方式是诚实的：低于方法论原文的 0.68，与已发表应用相当（0.2 与 0.39；另一项研究是 0.09 至 0.22），并且指出那些论文的作者也没有声称自己的 rho 足够大。

地方层面的公正治理 rho 只有 0.05，作者的原话是“tends to be too sensitive to make a conclusion”。这一条不是被审稿人挑出来的，是作者自己写进去的。这就是这一页最该讲的地方。

2SLS：针对最可能内生的两条中介（政治效能、反腐满意），排除性约束分别取民族主义、政治知识、腐败普遍性感知。效能到中央信任显著，反向不显著；反腐满意与中央信任互为因果，但前向效应约为反向的三倍。

省级固定效应下唯一的变化是公正治理对中央信任变得显著（p=0.032）。

Conservative by Design, Honest About Limits

Conservative choices are part of the argument

  • Provincial Gini flattens within-province variation
  • Social desirability pushes answers upward
  • The 2015 campaign compressed the variance in trust

→ The effects found are the lower bounds

The first two make the estimate at least an underestimate, and the third makes the effect harder to detect.

Limits stated up front

  • The total effect is not significant
  • Sequential ignorability is untestable (Imai, Keele, and Tingley 2010; Wolak 2020)
  • Replication materials are public

Proportion

Causally informed inference, not causal inference.

总效应不显著这件事，作者在附录里正面处理：中介分析中并不罕见 (Agler and De Boeck 2017)；总效应的标准误同时吸收直接与间接两部分的方差，因此间接效应的检验反而可能更有力 (Kenny and Judd 2014)。Kenny 与 Judd 的比方是：“It might be very hard to throw a ball 70 m in one throw. However, it is a lot easier to throw the ball 70 m in two throws.” 并且在单中介设定下总效应确实显著。

序贯可忽略性不可检验。作者引 Wolak 的表述，提醒“one needs to be very cautious to interpret the results of this research causally”，并承认用控制变量做的调整很难完备。

这一页建议放慢，它是整场演讲在方法上的可信度来源。愿意讲自己的下界和边界的研究，才值得被相信。

Estimating Inequality and Meritocracy

Target (Solt et al. 2016)

Which statement do you agree with:

  • Most people who want to get ahead can make it if they’re willing to work hard
  • Hard work and determination are no guarantee of success for most people

Model: multilevel logistic regression, individuals nested in counties, with both the intercept and the coefficient for income allowed to vary across counties

\[ \begin{align} \color{red}{Reject}\thinspace Meritocracy_{ij} =\boldsymbol{X\gamma} &+ \gamma_{10}Income_{ij} + \gamma_{01}Inequality_{j}\\ &+ \gamma_{11}Inequality_{j} \times Income_{ij} + u_{ij} \end{align} \]

Conditional effect

\[ \frac{\partial\thinspace Reject\thinspace Meritocracy}{\partial\thinspace Inequality} = \gamma_{01} + \gamma_{11}Income \]

收入按 1 至 9 编码（低于 1 万美元至高于 15 万美元）。县级控制变量：平均收入、黑人人口占比、2004 年布什得票率、总人口。个体控制变量：年龄、教育、性别、种族、公民身份、党派认同、意识形态、教会出席频率。缺失值用 R 包 mi 多重插补 (Rubin 1987; Su et al. 2011)。

不平等测量的局限，作者自己写在脚注里：ACS 县级 Gini 的福利定义是转移支付后、税前收入。由于大量再分配通过税法实现，税后指标更可取，但美国在低于全国的地理层级上几乎没有税后收入分布数据。

Measurement in the Chinese Case (Jin et al. 2024)

Data

CHFS 2013

  • 28,141 households, 267 counties, 29 provinces
  • Used only to construct the provincial Gini (0.49 to 0.66)

CUGS 2015

  • 3,513 respondents, 50 prefectures
  • Representative of urban citizens nationwide
  • Supplies mediators, outcomes, individual controls

Measurement

  • Impartial governance ← perceived equality of government treatment
  • Responsiveness ← external political efficacy
  • Judicial credibility ← trust in the fairness of courts and procuratorates
  • Anti-corruption ← satisfaction with anti-corruption efforts
  • Outcome: trust in the central and in the local government, 1 to 4

省级而非市级是数据所限，不是设计选择：CHFS 脱敏后无法在更低层级计算 Gini。作者明确指出，更同质、更平的省级测量很可能导致对不平等影响的低估。

信任的描述统计：中央均值 3.27（标准差 0.64），地方 2.98（标准差 0.75）。分层信任的格局在中国政治中是稳定的 (Li 2004)。

个体控制变量：性别、年龄、城镇化、教育、流动身份、家庭收入、职业类型、党员身份。情境控制变量：本地 GDP、人口、平均工资、政府财政收入。

Benchmarks

  • The original methodological paper: 0.68 (Imai, Keele, and Tingley 2010)
  • Published applications: 0.2 and 0.39 (Imai et al. 2011); 0.09 to 0.22 (Wolak 2020)
  • In none of those did the authors claim their rho was large enough

作者的结论措辞：在偏离中介可忽略性的一定范围内，结论仍可成立；但没有关于 rho 多大才算足够大的清晰标准或经验法则。

地方层面的公正治理是唯一被作者主动排除的路径，理由是过于敏感。由于他们本来就没有在该路径上提出实质发现，这不损害整体论证。

Other robustness checks

  • Province fixed effects: no substantive differences
    • Only change: impartial governance turns significant for central trust (p = 0.032)
  • Single-mediator models: all four paths and the total effect significant
  • Income subgroups: no conclusive differences
  • Urban sampling balance: 0.563 vs 0.569 (p = 0.467)

多重共线性：Kendall’s tau 中，公正治理与政治效能 0.62、与司法公正 0.52，司法与效能 0.46；反腐满意与其余仅 0.25 至 0.27。作者的处理是正文保留保守的全中介设定，附录报告单中介设定，并说明在现有数据中找不到既能避开共线性又能控制其他中介混淆的工具。

社会赞许性无法检验，作者的论证是信任类问题不涉及受访者自身行为，作答相对诚实；且任何偏误都推向更正面的回答和更小的方差，因此测到的负效应是低估。
